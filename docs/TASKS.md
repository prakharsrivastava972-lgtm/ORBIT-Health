# Daily tasks and gamification

All demo data is SIMULATED. Points and badges reward LOGGING, never results.

- Nine daily tasks: vitals (HR, BP, temperature, SpO2), glucose, sleep, fluid intake, weight, behavioral check-in,
  and three self-reported tasks (exercise, nutrition, stress break).
- Measurement and check-in tasks complete from stored data dated today. Deleting the entry un-completes the task.
- Points are recomputed from completions (10 per measurement or self task, 15 for the check-in), so they cannot drift.
- A streak day needs the check-in and at least one vital. An unfinished today does not break the streak.
- Milestones: first reading, first check-in, 3 and 7 day streaks, 10 and 25 days logged, mission 25/50/75/100%.
